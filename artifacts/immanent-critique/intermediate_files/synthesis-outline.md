## Global Rules for All Writers

- **INCOMPLETE entries** (keywords contain `INCOMPLETE`): cite them only to attribute a position or name a primary text. Any substantive account of what they argue must come from a complete entry that reports it. Each section gives the "grounding" entry to use. Never quote or paraphrase an INCOMPLETE annotation as if it were the source's own argument.
- **LOOSE-USE entries** (Domain 7): present them as examples of terminological drift, not as contributions to the method.
- **Terminology**: keep each author's own vocabulary. "Immanent" vs "internal" is itself contested (Stahl, Sabia, Walzer, Jaeggi use the terms differently). "Colonization" in Habermas/Stahl 2020 means colonization of the lifeworld, not colonialism. Do not run the two together.
- **Thread through every section**: the four axes from the plan, which are (a) source of standards, (b) role of contradiction, (c) reconstructive vs transformative, (d) relation to progress. Each section should say briefly where its figures fall on these axes. The Conclusion then gathers the results.
- **Gaps to state honestly**: Allen's *The End of Progress* (2016) has no book entry. Reach it through `leeb2018liberating`, `wiersma2026progress`, `solinas2025goodbyehegel`, `agatensi2025critique` and `volpe2025progress`. Horkheimer's "Traditional and Critical Theory" and Adorno's *Negative Dialectics* / *Minima Moralia* are not in the bibliography as entries. Refer to them only through the secondary entries.
- Tone: descriptive and analytical. No evaluative superlatives.

## Section 1: Introduction

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-2.bib, literature-domain-4.bib
**Word Target**: 550-650

**Purpose**: Define immanent critique in a preliminary way, set out the two-part structure, and flag that both the definition and the genealogy are disputed.

**Content**:
- Working definition: critique that takes its standards from the object criticised, not from outside it (Antonio 1981 calls this the core of Critical Theory). Also give the three-way contrast (external / internal / immanent) as Stahl draws it in `stahl2013habermas`, where immanent norms "point beyond" what participants already accept.
- First complication: the received "Continuity Thesis" (Hegel invents the method, Marx develops it, Adorno refines it) is rejected by `finlayson2014hegel`. The Introduction should say that Part I tells the story while keeping this challenge in view.
- Second complication: recent analytic scepticism. `fraser2023limits` argues the prospects are "bleak". `procyshyn2019conditions` argues immanent critique "is not a method in any strict sense".
- Preview the four axes and the section plan.

**Key Papers**: antonio1981immanent, finlayson2014hegel, stahl2013habermas, procyshyn2019conditions, fraser2023limits

## PART I: GENEALOGY

## Section 2: Determinate Negation and the Critique of Political Economy: Hegel, the Young Hegelians, Marx

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-1.bib, literature-domain-4.bib (becker2018immanent, buchwalter1991hegel, finlayson2014hegel), literature-domain-2.bib (antonio1981immanent)
**Word Target**: 950-1,050

**Section Purpose**: Set out the conceptual inheritance: standards generated by the object, determinate negation, contradiction. Then show that already in Marx this inheritance divides into categorial-immanent readings and moral-external readings.

**2.1 Hegel: criterion-testing and determinate negation** (~400 words)
- Core: pippin1989hegels (Hegel continues Kant's critical project, and the self-testing of forms of consciousness happens without external metaphysics); westphal2015hegels (a pragmatic-realist reconstruction of Kant, so the critique is reconstructive rather than external); becker2018immanent (two senses of immanent critique in the Phenomenology Introduction: judging an object by its own norms vs accounting for one's own standpoint); rosen1982determinate (a sceptical benchmark: can criticism with borrowed standards yield a positive result legitimately?).
- Optional: westphal2009blackwell (competing readings of the Introduction); sedgwick1993pippin (whether Hegel's critique of Kant is internal).
- INCOMPLETE, attribution only: pinkard1994hegels (social reading), maker1994philosophy (non-foundationalist reading). Mention them as further interpretive options. Do not develop their arguments.
- Connection: the Logic-vs-Phenomenology question (conceptual self-development vs criteria from consciousness) sets up the later split between "the standard is in the object" and "the critical practice itself is immanent" (picked up in Section 5 via `sarkela2017immanent`).

**2.2 Young Hegelians and the early Marx** (~200 words)
- Core: breckman1998marx (the political dimension of Young Hegelian critique); brudney1998marxs (Marx's attempt to "leave philosophy" while retaining a notion of the good life); buchwalter1991hegel (Marx keeps Hegel's unity of reason and reality, criticises its dogmatic form, and calls the result immanent critique, as opposed to utopian critique by "alien principles").
- Optional: leopold2007young (flourishing as an implicit standard).

**2.3 Capital's method and the Marx-and-justice debate** (~400 words)
- Core: postone1993time (critique of capitalism's historically specific forms; rejects the critique from the "standpoint of labour"); moseley2014marxs (the range of positions on Capital and Hegel's Logic); caligaris2014which (the "rational kernel" debate); ninos2024hegels (Marx's critique of Ricardo modelled on Hegel's immanent critique of finite cognition); geras2020controversy (survey of the Wood-Husami controversy: Marx condemns capitalism as unjust while disowning the language of justice).
- Optional: smith2014hegel (systematic dialectic); buchanan1981marxian (an analytic sceptic).
- INCOMPLETE, attribution only: wood1980marxian, wood1980right, husami1980marx. Present their positions **as Geras's abstract reports the two cases** ("the case for thinking he does not and the case for thinking that he does"). heinrich2012introduction and arthur2002new are named only as representatives of the Neue Marx-Lektüre and the "new dialectic" as grouped in `moseley2014marxs`.
- Tension to state: if Marx's critique needs a moral standard (Geras), is it still immanent? This question returns in Section 6 with Cohen.

**Section Summary**: The Hegelian-Marxian line gives critique a standard internal to its object. Already here, however, readers disagree on whether that standard is logical-categorial (Logic, systematic dialectic), social-historical, or covertly moral.

## Section 3: Negativity, Totality and the Self-Critique of Reason: First-Generation Critical Theory

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-2.bib, literature-domain-4.bib (finlayson2014hegel), literature-domain-6.bib (morris1996performative)
**Word Target**: 950-1,050

**Section Purpose**: Show how the first generation radicalises immanent critique into the critique of a totality and of reason itself. This creates the problems of standpoint and performative contradiction that the later sections inherit.

**3.1 From reification to critical theory** (~250 words)
- Core: gindner2023totality (Lukács's totality, read negatively); chari2010toward (Lukács's practice-oriented critique of reification vs Honneth's recognition reformulation); brincat2016traditional (Horkheimer's traditional/critical distinction, which also prepares the IR applications in Section 7).
- Optional: reynolds2023critique (Popper vs Adorno: critique as social rationality vs critique of social rationality).

**3.2 Adorno: immanent critique, non-identity, constellation** (~450 words)
- Core: oconnor2004adorno (non-identity as the epistemic ground of critical rationality); stone2014adorno (Adorno keeps Hegelian dialectic but changes reconciliation, and on this basis can criticise enlightenment consistently); athanassiadis2026hegel (contests Adorno's charge that Hegel's dialectic is identitarian); helmling2003constellation and feldman2011not (the Adorno-Benjamin tension over constellation, dialectical image and mediation); finlayson2014hegel (doubts that Adorno endorses "immanent criticism" as a description of his own practice); martinson2014adorno (immanent critique as performance); skirke2024phenomenology (reconstructs the "mediation argument" against phenomenology and contests it).
- Optional: ziegelmann2025immanent (the "standpoint of redemption" read as compatible with immanent critique; save the detail for Section 6).
- INCOMPLETE, attribution only: buckmorss1977origin, bernstein2001adorno, jarvis1998adorno.

**3.3 Marcuse, and the Dialectic of Enlightenment problem** (~300 words)
- Core: kellner1984herbert (Marcuse's reconstruction of Marxism; the unrealised potential of technology and freedom) vs gonzalez2018marcuse (one-dimensionality as a repudiation of dialectics); horkheimer2020dialectic (enlightenment reverts to myth); cook2004adorno (defends Adorno against Habermas: the standard is reason's unrealised potential); finlayson2003theory (a qualified defence of Habermas's critique of Adorno's ideology critique).
- Habermas's performative-contradiction charge: cite habermas1985philosophical (INCOMPLETE) as the locus, but **ground the characterisation in** `finlayson2009morality` (quotes Habermas on "normative foundations"), `stone2014adorno` and `morris1996performative`.
- Optional: kleinberg2021one; hammer2025routledge.

**Section Summary**: The first generation leaves two legacies. One is a negativist immanent critique of a "wrong" whole. The other is the charge that such critique cannot account for its own normative basis. The charge was first put by Habermas and is reconstructed by `finlayson2009morality`.

## Section 4: Reconstruction, Recognition, Connection, and the Methodological Turn (Habermas to Jaeggi and Stahl)

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-3.bib, literature-domain-4.bib, literature-domain-6.bib (ng2018social)
**Word Target**: 1,150-1,250

**Section Purpose**: Present the main post-1970 grounding strategies, and then the post-2000 theorists who made immanent critique an explicit object of theory. Exposition only. Section 5 handles the disputes.

**4.1 Habermas and Benhabib: communicative reason** (~300 words)
- Core: stahl2013habermas (against Habermas's own disclaimers, the Theory of Communicative Action is a "practice-based" immanent critique, and it rejects both transcendental and merely hermeneutic grounds); kompridis2006critique (critique as world-disclosure against Habermasian proceduralism).
- INCOMPLETE, attribution only: habermas1984theory (characterise via `stahl2013habermas`); benhabib1986critique. Describe Benhabib's two moments, "explanatory-diagnostic" and "anticipatory-utopian", **only as the terminology is used in** `allen2015emancipation` and `schmid2017poverty`. Do not claim more about her book.

**4.2 Honneth: normative reconstruction and its critics** (~350 words)
- Core: honneth2014freedom; repa2023reconstructive (the "surplus of validity" as transcendence within reality, against Jaeggi's classification of Honneth as "internal"); buchwalter2016concept (Hegel's reconstruction is "more robustly reconstructive and more robustly normative" than Honneth's; "immanent transcendence"); schaub2015misdevelopments (the method gives up radical critique); freyenhagen2015honneth (charges Honneth with reformism); mcneill2015social (lacks Hegel's justification of institutional rationality); carleheden2020criticize (reconstruction vs construction vs genealogy).
- Optional: zurn2000anthropology (early anthropological ground).
- INCOMPLETE: honneth2009reconstructive / honneth2001reconstructive. Cite as the "genealogical proviso" essay, characterised only through `carleheden2020criticize` and `repa2023reconstructive`.

**4.3 Fraser: dualism and crisis; Walzer: connected criticism** (~250 words)
- Core: thompson2005redistribution (Fraser's perspectival dualism vs Honneth's monism); fraser2017behind (an expanded concept of capitalism and its crises); oKane2021critical (Fraser's account is internally inconsistent); allen1998situated (Walzer/Rorty "loyal" critic); gregory2010political (Walzer relies on a tacit Kantian self).
- INCOMPLETE: fraser2003redistribution, fraser2018capitalism (cite for attribution, ground via Thompson); walzer1987interpretation (cite as the text, ground via `allen1998situated`, `gregory2010political`, `agnafors2012reassessing`).
- Optional: forst2011ground (a justification-based alternative; also used in Section 5).

**4.4 The methodological turn since 2000** (~350 words)
- Core: jaeggi2018critique (forms of life as problem-solving; crises; learning; "dialectical pragmatism"); jaeggi2009rethinking (ideology critique revived as social critique); celikates2018critique (critique as the social practice of ordinary agents; non-paternalistic, dialogical); celikates2012naoreconhecimento (between Bourdieu and Boltanski: no epistemic authority for participants "immune" to theory); sabia2010defending (immanent critique as hermeneutic, second-order critique; denies the relativism charge); ng2018social (Honneth's social freedom assessed by Mills's Marxian ideology critique).
- INCOMPLETE: stahl2013what, stahl2013immanente (cite as monograph and working paper; describe the ontology/epistemology/authority triad **via** `stahl2013habermas`'s abstract); ng2015ideology; boltanski2006justification (name only).

**Section Summary**: By the 2010s the field contains reconstructive (Habermas, Honneth), crisis-theoretic (Fraser, Jaeggi), hermeneutic (Walzer, Sabia) and reflexive-pragmatist (Celikates) variants. All claim immanence, and none agrees with the others on what makes a standard immanent.

## PART II: CONTEMPORARY DEBATES

## Section 5: What Makes Critique Immanent? Taxonomy, Normative Authority, and the Conservatism Objection

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-4.bib, literature-domain-5.bib, literature-domain-3.bib (raz1991morality, gregory2010political, forst2011ground)
**Word Target**: 1,200-1,300

**Section Purpose**: Treat the central objection: immanent critique either defers to existing norms (conservatism) or quietly imports external ones (covert externalism). Show how taxonomies, analytic parallels and realist debates reproduce the same dilemma.

**Main Claims**:
1. The immanent/internal/external distinction is drawn in at least three non-equivalent ways (where the standard is located; the form of the practice; a set of requirements).
2. Recent analytic work frames the conservatism objection as a metanormative dilemma. The responses relocate immanence to contradiction, experience or partisanship.
3. Analytic and realist literatures on internal reasons, reflective equilibrium and legitimacy face structurally the same dilemma.

**5.1 Taxonomies and their instability** (~350 words)
- Core: stahl2013habermas (ontology, epistemology, authority); sarkela2017immanent (immanence as a quality of the standard vs as the form of critical practice, Hegel/Dewey); procyshyn2019conditions (inherence, contradiction, access; heuristic and ampliative); becker2018immanent (two definitions); solinas2019political (Jaeggi's objections to Walzer's internal critique are inconsistent, and the dispute shows a "political deficit").

**5.2 The dilemma: conservatism or loss of bite** (~450 words)
- Core: fraser2023limits (implausible social ontology or flawed metanormativity); vonsamson2025immanent (the "collapse assumption" produces the dilemma, so immanence should be relocated to clarifying contradictions); evans2022normative (normative foundations are insoluble, by analogy with the "given"); stahl2024oppressive (oppressive forms of life distort the interpretations that constitute norms; answers via constitutive freedom or a partisan stance); marchettoni2019practices (Jaeggi's second-order problems need something like an abstract standard or telos); bodde2020lebensform (organicism limits critique of capitalism and bureaucracy; calls for a hybrid external-immanent critique).
- Optional: stahl2017particular (particular moral experience as a negative resource); decaux2024antinomic (antinomic normativity, a reply to Evans's scepticism).
- Hermeneutic strand: raz1991morality, gregory2010political, agnafors2012reassessing (Walzer as wide reflective equilibrium).

**5.3 Analytic and realist parallels** (~400 words)
- Core: williams1981internal (internal reasons, with the same recognisability constraint and the same conservatism worry); daniels1996justice (wide reflective equilibrium as coherentist internal justification); cohen2008rescuing (fact-insensitive ultimate principles, the strongest case for external standards) vs rossi2016facts (realist defence of fact-sensitivity); rorty1989contingency / rorty1990ethnocentrism (owning ethnocentrism, conventionalism made explicit); geuss2008philosophy and prinz2016geuss (realism as a radicalisation of critical purpose); cozzaglio2020political (internalist legitimacy needs room for external critique).
- Genealogy as the non-foundational alternative: hanpile2016foucault (refuses the demand for justification; replies to crypto-normativity); allen2014normative (Koopman's genealogy as critique).
- Optional: dworkin2011justice (constructive interpretation with holistic justification); forst2011ground (right to justification as a context-transcending ground).

**Section Summary**: No party has dissolved the dilemma. The live options are: (i) relocate immanence (to contradiction, practice form or experience); (ii) accept a hybrid with external elements (Cozzaglio, Bodde, Forst); (iii) reject the demand for foundations (Evans, Han-Pile).

## Section 6: Contradiction, Ideology, and Immanent Transcendence: From Ideology Critique to "Wrong Life"

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-6.bib, literature-domain-5.bib (aytac2022ideology, sleat2024against, clark2024moderate, rossi2024critical), literature-domain-2.bib (freyenhagen2013adorno, freyenhagen2017whole, finlayson2002adorno, ziegelmann2025immanent, decaux2024antinomic), literature-domain-4.bib (sorensen2022critical, vonsamson2025immanent), literature-domain-3.bib (repa2023reconstructive)
**Word Target**: 1,150-1,250

**Section Purpose**: Show how immanent critique works in practice. What "contradiction" means, whether ideology critique is a kind of immanent critique, and how critique can transcend its context without becoming external.

**6.1 Is ideology critique immanent critique?** (~450 words)
- Core: bianchin2019ideology (Jaeggi's immanent ideology critique cashed out through functional explanation and stability); haslanger2012resisting and haslanger2017racism (ideology as practices and shared dispositions, vs Shelby's cognitivism; an ameliorative, partly external base); morris2016knowledge (Marxian ideology critique as a theory of knowledge, against pure debunking); stahl2022ideological (ideal theory is ideological in form, not content: concepts presented as practice-independent); sorensen2022critical (immanent critique is not ideology critique).
- Realist ideology critique: aytac2022ideology (genealogical, epistemic, no morality) vs sleat2024against (morality is indispensable); clark2024moderate (the epistemology dilemma; a moderate version); rossi2024critical; stanley2015propaganda with rossi2017stanley (moralised vs de-moralised democracy).
- Optional: pearson2025crisis (limits of debunking under crisis); bufkin2024racism.
- INCOMPLETE, attribution only: shelby2003ideology (characterise via `haslanger2017racism`); sankaran2019new; mills2018rethinking.

**6.2 Contradiction: real, performative, practical** (~250 words)
- Core: vonsamson2025immanent (immanent contradictions as the method's specificity); morris1996performative (a performative contradiction need not mean incoherence); procyshyn2019conditions (contradiction as a requirement); fraser2017behind (crisis-generating contradictions of capitalism's background conditions).

**6.3 Immanent transcendence and utopia** (~200 words)
- Core: repa2023reconstructive (the surplus of validity); strydom2022critical (immanent-transcendence and possibility); stahl2023beyond (critical theory needs a utopian dimension because non-ideal circumstances distort ideals); allen2015emancipation (negativistic emancipation; the paradox of power and emancipation).
- Optional: cooke2021immanent. INCOMPLETE: levitas1991educated (Bloch named only).

**6.4 "Wrong life" and negativism** (~300 words)
- Core: freyenhagen2013adorno (negative Aristotelianism; living "less wrongly"); finlayson2002adorno (a minimal negative ethics; the ineffable); freyenhagen2011ethics (ethics without the ineffable); celikates2017critique (Freyenhagen underestimates Adorno's immanent critique); oconnor2017interests (interests imply a standard of the good); freyenhagen2017whole (replies to Allen, Celikates and O'Connor); whyman2016wrong (wrong life read as despair; Hegelian holism inverted); skirke2008actions (wrong life as a claim about moral reality); ziegelmann2025immanent ("standpoint of redemption" as "inverse utopianism").
- Optional: reeves2016beyond; decaux2024antinomic. INCOMPLETE: butler2015good (attribution only).

**Section Summary**: Contradiction-centred, ideology-centred and negativist versions locate critical force in different places: social antagonism, distorted belief-formation, and experienced wrongness. Whether this force can be had without a positive standard (O'Connor vs Freyenhagen) is still open.

## Section 7: Progress, Coloniality, Gender, and Immanent Critique Beyond Philosophy

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-7.bib, literature-domain-4.bib (radhakrishnan2025critique, wiersma2026progress, unal2025jaeggi, melo2025learning, moilanen2025immanent, allen2016progress), literature-domain-3.bib (agatensi2025critique), literature-domain-5.bib (santos2022genealogy, cozzaglio2022political), literature-domain-2.bib (feenberg2023marcuses, brincat2016traditional)

**Section Summary**: The progress objection hits reconstructive versions hardest (Kim, Solinas). Outside philosophy, uses split between theorised adaptations (Isaksen, Curtis, Dhillon) and loose labels.

## Section 8: Conclusion

**BibTeX files**: literature-domain-4.bib, literature-domain-5.bib, literature-domain-7.bib
**Word Target**: 500-600

**Content**:
- Restate the genealogy against Finlayson's challenge to the Continuity Thesis. What persists is a family of commitments (standards from the object, contradiction, transformation), not a single method (Procyshyn).
- Summarise the four axes. (a) Source of standards: embedded norms, practice form, contradiction or experience. (b) Contradiction: central for Marx, Adorno, Fraser and von Samson; displaced by "surplus" in Honneth. (c) Reconstructive vs transformative: Honneth and Habermas vs Jaeggi and Adorno. (d) Progress: the most exposed point (Allen, Solinas).
- Unresolved: the conservatism/externalism dilemma (Fraser 2023, von Samson); whose context counts (Radhakrishnan, Stahl 2024); little contact with the analytic internal-reasons literature (Williams, Cohen); German-language core texts are not in the bibliography.
- Possible directions the literature itself points to: hybrid external-immanent critique (Bodde, Cozzaglio); pluralism about methods (Radhakrishnan, Carleheden); critique as a practice of agents (Celikates, Särkelä).

**Key Papers**: finlayson2014hegel, procyshyn2019conditions, vonsamson2025immanent, fraser2023limits, radhakrishnan2025critique, bodde2020lebensform
