DOMAIN_OVERVIEW:
Theoretical philosophy in 2016-2018 is marked by systematic reconstruction of Kant's metaphysics and account of cognition rather than commentary on single Critiques. Stang's Kant's Modal Metaphysics (2016) traces the pre-Critical and Critical theories of possibility; Willaschek's Kant on the Sources of Metaphysics (2018) reads the Transcendental Dialectic as explaining why reason generates metaphysical questions; Watkins and Willaschek's 'Kant's Account of Cognition' (2017) fixes the distinction between Erkenntnis and Wissen and the notion of givenness; and Pollok (2017) reads the critical turn as a normative turn. The conceptualism versus nonconceptualism and perception debates continue through Schulting's Kantian Nonconceptualism (2016), Conant (2016), McLear (2016), the O'Shea Critical Guide and the Gomes and Stephenson volume (both 2017), with Longuenesse's I, Me, Mine (2017) on self-consciousness. Philosophy of science and logic are represented by Massimi and Breitenbach's Kant and the Laws of Nature (2017), McNulty on chemistry in the Opus postumum (2016), Sutherland on number (2017) and Lu-Adler's Kant and the Science of Logic (2018); the life sciences by Nassar (2016) and Huneman (2017).

In practical philosophy the block includes Guyer's Virtues of Freedom (2016), Hoewing's volume on the highest good (2016), Kleingeld on the Formula of Universal Law (2017), Merritt on reflection and virtue (2018), Papish on evil and self-deception (2018), the Cambridge Texts revised Metaphysics of Morals (2017) and new edition of Religion (2018), and Thorndike on the late transition project (2017). Korsgaard's Fellow Creatures (2018) is the main Kantian contribution on animals. Political and legal work includes Rauscher's Cambridge Edition volume of political lectures and drafts (2016), Westphal on Hume and Kant and natural law (2016), Flikschuh on global orientation (2017) and Holtman on welfare (2018). The race, colonialism and gender debate is represented by Allais (2016), Valdez (2017), Varden on women (2017), and Mills and Sandford (2018). Reception is covered by Matherne on Merleau-Ponty (2016), Engelland on Heidegger (2017) and Nisenbaum on German Idealism (2018); reference work is limited to the Kant-Kongress volume Natur und Freiheit (2018).

Year dates follow the CrossRef print date. Online-first items whose print year falls in the block (for example Varden, Valdez, Sandford, Huneman) are dated by the print volume; Denis's revised Metaphysics of Morals and the 2018 Religion edition are dated by CrossRef print date (online dates differ). Each case is explained in the entry's YEAR NOTE.

RELEVANCE_TO_PROJECT:
The block documents the shift from commentary on the Critiques toward systematic reconstruction of Kant's metaphysics, cognition and normativity, the consolidation of the perception and nonconceptualism debates, the first book-length treatments of Kant's logic and of the Dialectic's sources, and the growth of race, colonialism, gender and animal-ethics work as a recognised strand of Kant scholarship.
