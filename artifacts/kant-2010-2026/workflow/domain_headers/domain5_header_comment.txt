DOMAIN_OVERVIEW:
The three years combine continuity with the second-generation systematic readings of the 2010s and a visible shift of attention to Kant's racism, to imagination and intuition, and to late and early work. Theoretical philosophy is represented by the metaphysical turn (Schafer and Stang's volume on the sensible and intelligible worlds, 2022; Schafer on Kant's conception of reason, 2023; Gomes, Moore and Stephenson on the necessity of the categories, 2022), by the conceptualism debate (Pendlebury 2022, Kinzel 2024 on Marburg neo-Kantianism), by schematism (Stang 2023), by the two-aspect debate argued from Kant's language (Rosefeldt 2024), and by two books on the faculties of sensibility and imagination (Smyth on intuition and Matherne on imagination, both 2024, both with NDPR reviews). Science and the late work are covered by McNulty's Critical Guide to the Metaphysical Foundations and Basile and Lyssy on the Opus postumum (2022), Cooper on natural history and Howard on the Opus postumum (2023), and Aigner on Kant and technics (2024).

Practical philosophy shows a turn to the Doctrine of Virtue and moral psychology (Timmons 2022, Herman 2022, Rinne and Brecher 2023, Vilhauer 2024), to the formulas of the categorical imperative (Fahmy 2023, Kleingeld 2023) and to freedom and agency (Kohl 2023), and to the reception of Kantian ethics (Guyer 2024 on Kant's impact on moral philosophy, Ameriks 2024 on dignity). Political philosophy is led by work on independence, labour and citizenship (Pascoe 2022, Stone and Hasan 2022, Davies 2023) and by Guyer's Element on the moral foundation of Right (2024). The Kant-and-race debate moves from papers on savagery and the travel reports (Lu-Adler 2022) to Lu-Adler's book Kant, Race, and Racism (2023) and to work that ties race to the Religion and to the Haitian Revolution (Papish 2024, Huseyinzadegan 2024), with Ameriks 2024 treating Kant's prejudices as an obstacle to his own account of dignity. Religion is covered by Rosen 2022, Chignell 2023 and Papish 2024; aesthetics and teleology by Geiger 2022, Sweet 2023 and Clewis 2023, with Matherne and Russell 2024 on imagination and fantasy; reception by Lambeth 2023 (Heidegger), Kinzel 2024 (Marburg), Guyer 2024 and Aigner 2024.

The tercentenary year yields the Oxford Handbook of Kant (CrossRef print date 2024-10-31) and an Oxford and Cambridge output of single-author books and Elements, but no new Cambridge Edition volume was found. Walschots's Background Source Materials for the second Critique (2024, NDPR review) is the main edition-type entry; Kant congresses and special issues for the anniversary were found mainly in Romanian, Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian and Iberian journals (for example the Kant 300 PT dossier in Con-Textos Kantianos, Revue roumaine de philosophie, Sententiae) and were not included. Dates follow the CrossRef print date. Online-first items whose print year falls in the block (Pendlebury, Timmons, Stang, Fahmy, Kinzel, among others) carry their DOI in the url field because the metadata cleaner would otherwise overwrite the year with the earlier online year; each such case is explained in the entry's YEAR NOTE. Plan seeds were checked as follows: Lu-Adler's Kant and the Science of Logic is 2018 (Domain 3); Sutherland's Kant's Mathematical World is 2021 (Domain 4); McNulty (2022), Lu-Adler's Kant, Race, and Racism (2023), the Oxford Handbook of Kant (2024) and Baiasu and Timmons, The Kantian Mind (CrossRef online date 2023-06-22, no print date) were verified; Massimi's Perspectival Realism (OUP 2022) was verified but screened out as not centred on Kant.

RELEVANCE_TO_PROJECT:
The block records a Kant literature with more attention to imagination, intuition, the Opus postumum and the Baumgarten background, a mature race debate with defenders, critics and a book-length case, the first Kant-and-technology entries, and a reception strand that extends from Heidegger to Marburg neo-Kantianism and contemporary moral philosophy. Because citation counts lag, significance rests on publisher and series, NDPR and journal reviews and SEP citations, and these are recorded in each entry.
